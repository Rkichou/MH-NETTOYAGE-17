# Hero photographique

Reference de composition : capture fournie par le client du design Breezy,
Wolfpixel UI/UX, Dribbble shot 24894700. Aucun fichier de cette maquette n'est
repris dans le site. La palette, le logo et les textes sont ceux de MH Nettoyage 17.

Source de l'image : `public/images/volkswagen-golf-apres-lavage-vue-trois-quarts.jpeg`.
Asset final : `public/images/hero-golf-detouree.webp`.
Outil : image_gen integre, mode edition / background-extraction ; compression WebP
avec Sharp, qualite 88, alpha conserve. La photo originale reste inchangee.
Le detourage et la correction de couleur sont generatifs : le resultat est un
visuel retouche, pas un document avant/apres ni une preuve de resultat.

## Prompt utilise

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied real photograph of a white Volkswagen Golf. Create a precise clean transparent-background cutout of ONLY the exact car in this photograph for an editorial car wash website hero. Preserve the car identity, five-door body, wheels, Volkswagen badge, original three-quarter camera angle, white paint, headlights and real photographic reflections. Remove the entire garage, walls, shelves, cables, outdoor background and concrete floor. Keep ALL of the car and all wheels fully visible without cropping. Slightly level the car so it sits naturally. Keep natural photographic detail, not a 3D render, not an illustration. Neutralize the strong yellow indoor color cast lightly without repainting or changing details. License plate should be plain neutral without legible personal registration. Actual transparent alpha background, no checkerboard drawn into image, no white rectangular background, no text. Landscape image, car centered occupying 90 percent of image width, small transparent margins. No additional objects. Deliver a high resolution PNG cutout.
